monaco-helix

Selection-first editing for Monaco. Try e then c to change a word, x then d to delete a line, or v to extend a selection. i enters Insert; Esc returns to Normal.

Use / for a live search preview, Q to start or stop a macro, and q to replay it. Prefixes such as g, m, and Space show their available commands below the editor.
